Questions

What ages is this for?

Built for 2–5. The youngest target is 2-year-olds who can point a camera; the oldest is pre-K kids picking up early vocabulary in two languages. The audio-first design and giant tap targets are sized for that range.

Which languages can my kid learn?

Spanish, French, and Mandarin today, with native-quality audio. More languages are on the way. You pick the language; every prompt is then spoken in English and the language you chose, and the foreign word shows on screen.

Do I need to speak the language myself?

No. The app speaks both languages aloud in a clear, slow voice — you don't have to know any Spanish to hand your kid a Spanish lesson. Many parents pick up the words alongside their child.

Is it really one-time, not a subscription?

Really one-time. $19.99 unlocks everything, forever, on all your devices. We don't do recurring billing. No "cancel before the trial ends," no surprise renewal.

Does it use my kid's photos?

No. Photos go to Google's Gemini Vision API for one-time matching, then disappear. Nothing is stored, trained on, or shared. The app stores zero personal data — no signup, no account, no email collection. Progress lives in your browser.

Will the AI always get it right?

Mostly. Modern vision AI is impressive but not perfect — and a toddler's photos are hard (blurry, off-center, odd angles). We tuned it to be generous: a sippy cup counts as a cup, a stuffed lion counts as a lion. When it does miss, a parent-override button marks it correct. Better to celebrate than frustrate.

Does it work offline?

The app shell loads offline (good for spotty WiFi), but the AI photo-matching needs internet. If your kid snaps a photo while you're off-grid, they get a friendly "no internet" message.

Who built this?

One dad, one 3-year-old, one coffee. She loved it, and she's picking up Spanish words from it. We're seeing if other families want the same.
